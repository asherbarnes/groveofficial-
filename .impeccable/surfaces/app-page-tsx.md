## Direction contract

THESIS: The site is The Grove's own house with Showtime as a tenant brand. It refuses the generic "sports nonprofit" page (sage buttons, kicker labels over every heading, one accent everywhere) and instead makes the brand architecture visible: Grove surfaces are near-black, off-white and forest; amber appears only where Showtime is speaking.

OWN-WORLD: Near-black header and footer carrying the real Grove mark. Off-white reading surfaces, forest green as the Grove's field color for whole bands and its primary buttons, amber #ffb800 reserved for Showtime (the Programs hero field, Join Showtime buttons, Showtime dates). Oswald 600 condensed caps as stacked block headlines with tight but non-colliding leading; Source Sans 3 body. Short 48px rule motif in brand color (forest for Grove, amber for Showtime). Squared photos that truly bleed. One 2px-radius button family.

STORY: A visitor sees the mark on the wall and on the header and knows this is The Grove; understands it is a community nonprofit with its own facility; sees Showtime clearly framed as the training program inside it; and takes one of two doors (Join Showtime, Book the Space), or gives to the scholarship.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Near-black header (white mark plus THE GROVE, nav, amber Join Showtime). Hero: left half near-black with "THE GROVE." at display scale, the tagline, one paragraph, two buttons (amber Join Showtime, outline Book the Space), "San Jose · Est. 2017" meta below; right half the logo-wall team photo bleeding to the viewport edge.
